Optimize and Adapt

What my pilot is showing so far, what I want to change because of it, and how I will test those changes. My pilot, Skill Reinforcement through Therapeutic Activities, runs at A Parentz Touch ABA.

  • Project: Skill Reinforcement through Therapeutic Activities
  • Site: A Parentz Touch ABA
  • Supervisor: L'Tricia Turner
  • Pilot status: Week 9 of 15, two of three families still taking part

The short version

The program is effective when it is implemented consistently, and consistency has been the greatest challenge for families. Caregivers report that they find the program helpful and have requested additional guidance and examples, but the tracking log has not been maintained since the baseline week because it places too great a demand on them.

I initially expected the home to be the strongest setting. In practice, community outings to activities the children are motivated to do, such as Vegas Play Pen and a pumpkin patch, have produced a more positive response and allow me to coach caregivers in the moment.

I am proposing three changes: a brief checkbox sheet in place of the caregiver tracking log, a weekly check in message with one strategy and one example, and more trials during community outings. I will test them with one family first, revise them in Week 11, and then implement them with both families. My evaluation plan on the Monitor and Evaluate page already tracks caregiver logging, setting, and whether the work fits into each family's life, which is how I will monitor the changes.
