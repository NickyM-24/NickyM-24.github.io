Optimize and Adapt

What my pilot is showing so far, what I want to change because of it, and how I will test those changes. My pilot, Skill Reinforcement through Therapeutic Activities, runs at A Parentz Touch ABA.

  • Project: Skill Reinforcement through Therapeutic Activities
  • Site: A Parentz Touch ABA
  • Supervisor: L'Tricia Turner
  • Pilot status: Week 9 of 15, two of three families still taking part

The short version

The program works when it is used consistently, and consistency is the hard part for busy families. Caregivers like the program and have asked for more guidance and examples, but my tracking log has not been kept up since the baseline week because it asks too much of them.

I also expected home to be the strongest setting, but outings to places the children want to go, like Vegas Play Pen and a pumpkin patch, have gotten a more positive response, and they let me coach parents in the moment.

I am proposing three changes: a short checkbox sheet in place of the caregiver tracking log, a weekly check in text with one strategy reminder and one example, and more trials on community outings. I will try them with one family first, adjust in Week 11, and then use them with both families. My evaluation plan from Monitor and Evaluate already tracks caregiver logging and whether the work fits into a family's life, which is how I will know if the changes help.
