Recruitment Strategy

Recruitment strategy

Recruitment ran through a few channels: collaborating with social workers, counselors, and therapists at the agency who could introduce the survey to their clients; an online version of the survey with privacy and consent handled carefully given that most respondents would be minors; and direct engagement with foster parents and caregivers, who played a gatekeeping and support role in whether kids felt comfortable participating.

A few principles guided the approach throughout: build trust before asking anything sensitive, keep language age-appropriate, offer flexible formats (in person vs. online) so kids could choose what they were comfortable with, and be explicit that participation was voluntary and confidential.

In-person interviews were scheduled across several sessions in October 2023.

Interview guide

The interview goal was framed around enhancing foster parent and caregiver practices — using direct conversations with kids to understand what actually helps versus what doesn't, so that guidance to caregivers could be grounded in the kids' own perspective rather than assumptions.

Interview questions started broad (hobbies, a recent good memory) before moving into more sensitive territory (what triggers a tantrum, how it feels afterward, who they trust) — standard practice for building comfort before asking harder questions.

Opening and closing the interview

Every interview opened with a script thanking the participant, explaining that responses were confidential and no identifying information would be shared, and reminding them they could skip any question or stop at any time. It closed by summarizing what was shared, thanking them again, and leaving room for anything else they wanted to add.

Documentation approach

Interviews were documented through notes rather than video or audio, given the added consent complexity of recording minors in foster care. Notes-only was chosen as the most practical way to stay compliant with legal and ethical guidelines while still capturing accurate, reviewable detail.
