The outcomes

OutcomeWhat it asks
CC.A Effectively Establishes and Engages in Collaboration and Partnership OpportunitiesEffectively collaborate in professional contexts by establishing and maintaining formal partnerships to further educational initiatives.
CC.B Fosters Constructive Collaboration and PartnershipsWhen leading formal partnerships, support a constructive collaboration climate by effectively delegating and managing communication and tasks across a team; treating partners respectfully; conveying a positive attitude about the partnership and its work; motivating partners; and providing assistance and/or encouragement to partners.
CC.C Uses Sources and Evidence Effectively in CommunicationOrganize and synthesize high-quality, credible, relevant sources to develop a presentation that is appropriate to the discipline, format, and audience.
CC.D Establishes Context of and Purpose for Professional CommunicationDemonstrate a thorough understanding of context, audience, and purpose that is responsive to the assigned communication/presentation task(s). (Can be verbal or written.)
CC.E Uses Technology for Communication and CollaborationChoose and use technology tools in ways that enhance meaning and purpose for communication and/or collaboration.
