Recruitment strategy

Recruitment ran through a few channels: collaborating with social workers, counselors, and therapists at the agency who could introduce the survey to their clients; an online version of the survey with privacy and consent handled carefully given that most respondents would be minors; and direct engagement with foster parents and caregivers, who played a gatekeeping and support role in whether kids felt comfortable participating.

A few principles guided the approach throughout: build trust before asking anything sensitive, keep language age-appropriate, offer flexible formats (in person vs. online) so kids could choose what they were comfortable with, and be explicit that participation was voluntary and confidential.

In-person interviews were scheduled across several sessions in October 2023.
