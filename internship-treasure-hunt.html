Internship

Treasure Hunt

An early assignment researching the internship organization itself — Bamboo Sunrise, a Nevada-based foster care and youth social services agency in Henderson, founded in November 2011 by CEO Shirley Lim Holdeman.

Mission

Bamboo Sunrise focuses exclusively on Nevada's kids — not a national organization with a local branch, but a Nevada-founded agency built around giving local children a place to call home and the skills to become leaders in their community.

What they do

The agency supports safe, nurturing foster and adoptive families, working from the belief that protecting and strengthening families — birth, foster, and adoptive alike — takes a team with diverse skills working toward one shared vision. They explicitly reject the idea that poverty or homelessness alone should ever be a reason to separate a family.

How

Ongoing training and consultation for foster and adoptive families is central to how Bamboo Sunrise operates, done in collaboration with Child Welfare Services and partner organizations across public and private institutions.

Why it matters

The research surfaced a recurring idea worth carrying forward: the importance of reflecting on your own developmental needs before caring for someone else's — a kind of parallel process that shows up throughout child welfare work, and one that shaped how later parts of this project approached working with kids directly.
