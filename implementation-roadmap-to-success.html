Implementation

Roadmap to Success

My plan for implementing Skill Reinforcement through Therapeutic Activities at A Parentz Touch ABA, running the full length of the course from August 26 to December 4, 2026. Each week lists key activities and the milestones they build toward.

  • Project: Skill Reinforcement through Therapeutic Activities
  • Organization: A Parentz Touch ABA
  • Supervisor: L'Tricia Turner
  • Supervisor meetings: Tuesday, Wednesday, and Saturday
  • Course dates: August 26 – December 4, 2026

Project calendar

Phase 1 · Build and Prepare
Week 1 Aug 26, 2026
  • Meet with my supervisor to confirm scope, caseload, and consent requirements
  • Review my project plan from EDU 484
  • Revise my roadmap based on her feedback
Week 2 Sep 2, 2026
  • Build my activity set in age tiers, so each strategy has a younger and an older version
  • Identify which natural environments each strategy can be practiced in
Week 3 Sep 9, 2026
  • Write the caregiver handout in plain language, stating exactly what I am doing, why, and what outcome to look for
  • Get the handout approved
Week 4 Sep 16, 2026
  • Define what success looks like
  • Build a tracking sheet for frequency, duration, strategy used, and what helped
  • Get my tracking method approved
Week 5 Sep 23, 2026
  • Recruit 2 to 3 caregivers
  • Walk each caregiver through the strategies in detail so they can use them independently
  • Gather baseline information on each child's triggers and current strategies
MilestoneAge tiered materials built and approved. Caregivers recruited and trained on exactly what I am doing. Baseline data collected.
Phase 2 · Pilot in the Natural Environment
Week 6 Sep 30, 2026
  • Begin sessions in each child's own natural environment
  • Match the strategy level to where the child is in that moment
  • Log every session
Week 7 Oct 7, 2026
  • Caregiver present and practicing alongside me, so they can run it themselves afterward
  • Check what carried over between visits
Week 8 Oct 14, 2026
  • Continue sessions
  • Note which strategy level fits which child and how perception differs by age
Week 9 Oct 21, 2026
  • Continue sessions with caregivers leading where they are ready
  • Note where a caregiver still needs support
Week 10 Oct 28, 2026
  • Mid point review
  • Compare my data to baseline
  • Bring a written summary to my supervisor
MilestoneFive weeks of data from real environments. Caregivers running strategies independently between visits. Mid point review complete.
Phase 3 · Refine and Hand Off
Week 11 Nov 4, 2026
  • Revise activities based on my mid point data
  • Drop what did not work
  • Adjust the age tiers where a level did not match
Week 12 Nov 11, 2026
  • Run my revised sessions
  • Explain the changes to caregivers
Week 13 Nov 18, 2026
  • Ask caregivers directly whether they can run each strategy without me and what still feels unclear
  • Make final adjustments from their answers
Week 14 Nov 25, 2026
  • Write my caregiver facing implementation guide covering how to run each activity, which level to use, what to track, and what outcome to expect
  • Send the draft to my supervisor
Week 15 Dec 2, 2026
  • Final review, course ends Dec 4
  • Present my outcomes and guide to my supervisor
  • Decide what continues and who owns it
MilestoneCaregiver facing implementation guide complete. Each caregiver able to run at least one strategy independently. Handoff decided.

Roles and responsibilities

Nicky Malone

Project lead. I design the materials, run all sessions, collect and analyze the data, train caregivers, write the implementation guide, and revise the plan based on feedback.

L'Tricia Turner

My internship supervisor. She approves my project scope and tracking methods, advises me on clinical fit, and reviews my progress at our weekly meetings on Tuesday, Wednesday, and Saturday.

Caregivers and parents

The most important partners in this project. They are with the children the most, so they carry the strategies forward when I am not there. They learn each strategy alongside me and tell me what is working.

Clients and families

The people this project is for. They practice strategies in their own natural environment and their responses drive every revision I make.
