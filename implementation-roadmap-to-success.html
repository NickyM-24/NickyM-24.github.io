Implementation

Roadmap to Success

A three month plan for implementing Skill Reinforcement through Therapeutic Activities at A Parentz Touch ABA. Each week lists key activities and the milestones they build toward.

  • Project: Skill Reinforcement through Therapeutic Activities
  • Organization: A Parentz Touch ABA
  • Supervisor: L'Tricia Turner
  • Supervisor meetings: Tuesday, Wednesday, and Saturday

Project calendar

Month 1 · Build and Prepare
Week 1 Aug 29, 2026
  • Meet with supervisor to confirm scope, caseload, and consent requirements
  • Review project plan
  • Finalize roadmap
Week 2 Sep 5, 2026
  • Build activity set in age tiers, so each strategy has a younger and an older version
  • Identify which natural environments each strategy can be practiced in
Week 3 Sep 12, 2026
  • Write the caregiver handout in plain language, stating exactly what I am doing, why, and what outcome to look for
  • Define what success looks like
  • Build tracking sheet for frequency, duration, strategy used, and what helped
  • Get tracking method approved
Week 4 Sep 19, 2026
  • Recruit 2 to 3 caregivers
  • Walk each caregiver through the strategies in detail so they can use them independently
  • Gather baseline information on each child's triggers and current strategies
MilestoneAge tiered materials built and approved. Caregivers trained on exactly what I am doing. Baseline data collected.
Month 2 · Pilot in the Natural Environment
Week 5 Sep 26, 2026
  • Begin sessions in each child's own natural environment
  • Match the strategy level to where the child is in that moment
  • Log every session
Week 6 Oct 3, 2026
  • Continue sessions
  • Caregiver present and practicing alongside me, so they can run it themselves afterward
  • Check what carried over between visits
Week 7 Oct 10, 2026
  • Continue sessions
  • Note which strategy level fits which child and how perception differs by age
Week 8 Oct 17, 2026
  • Mid point review
  • Compare data to baseline
  • Bring written summary to supervisor
MilestoneFour weeks of data from real environments. Caregivers running strategies independently between visits.
Month 3 · Refine and Hand Off
Week 9 Oct 24, 2026
  • Revise activities based on mid point data
  • Drop what did not work
  • Adjust the age tiers where a level did not match
  • Explain changes to caregivers
Week 10 Oct 31, 2026
  • Run revised sessions
  • Ask caregivers directly whether they can run each strategy without me and what still feels unclear
Week 11 Nov 7, 2026
  • Write caregiver facing implementation guide covering how to run each activity, which level to use, what to track, and what outcome to expect
  • Send draft to supervisor for review
Week 12 Nov 14, 2026
  • Final review
  • Present outcomes and guide to supervisor
  • Decide what continues and who owns it
MilestoneCaregiver facing implementation guide complete. Each caregiver able to run at least one strategy independently. Handoff decided.

Roles and responsibilities

Nicky Malone

Project lead. I design the materials, run all sessions, collect and analyze the data, write the implementation guide, and revise the plan based on feedback.

L'Tricia Turner

Internship supervisor. Approves project scope and tracking methods, advises on clinical fit, and reviews progress at weekly Saturday meetings.

Caregivers

End users. Test coping strategies at home, report what carried over from sessions, and give feedback on whether the handout is clear and realistic.

Clients and families

The people this project is for. They participate in sessions and their responses drive every revision.
