Education in Society

How education functions within broader social, cultural, and political contexts. My Module 3 brainstorm connects three course experiences to this domain: EDU 301, Why Education?; my Universal Design evaluation of Knott's Berry Farm in LSE 401; and my EDU 484 human centered design research on violent tantrums among children in the foster system.

The short version

Looking at these three experiences together, my development in this domain moved outward. I began by studying the question of what education is for, then went out into a real public setting to see who that education was actually reaching, and finally worked directly with the people affected to build something with them rather than for them.

The most consistent lesson across all three was that my own assumptions were the first thing that needed examining, whether about where education happens, what it should accomplish for a particular child, or who is best positioned to say what works.
