Inspiration

Survey / Interview Guide

Foster Child's Perspective on Dealing with Violent Tantrums

Who the solution needed to serve

Three groups were identified as essential to talk to: foster parents and caregivers (day-to-day experience managing tantrums), child psychologists and therapists (the clinical/evidence-based lens), and social workers and child welfare professionals (the systemic and policy view). Hearing from all three — not just the clinical experts — was treated as essential to designing something that would actually work in practice.

Survey design

The survey ("Foster Child's Perspective on Dealing with Violent Tantrums") was built around 15 questions covering frequency, triggers, what helps during a tantrum, how tantrums are talked about afterward, and impact on school and relationships — plus a short demographic section (age, gender, time in foster care, number of placements) kept to the end, per standard survey design practice.

You can view and take the live form on the Survey page.

Recruitment approach

Recruitment leaned on relationships already in place — coordinating through social workers, therapists, and colleagues at the agency — with particular attention to consent and privacy given the population. Interviews were scheduled with youth participants and staff members at the group home in fall 2023, approached with the goal of creating a safe, non-judgmental space and letting participants set the pace of what they were comfortable sharing.
