Identity as an Educator

Who I am as an educator, where my beliefs come from, and where I am going. My Module 6 brainstorm covers Act of Learning and Identity as an Educator together.

  • Experience 1: EDU 304, my Empathy and Advocacy Case Study on Dr. Bruce D. Perry
  • Experience 2: EDU 302, my cultural autobiography and Cultural Equity Statement
  • Experience 3: EDU 498, preparing and leading my trauma informed pilot at A Parentz Touch ABA

The short version

One of my core beliefs is that children are not their behavior. In every setting, from a Montessori classroom to a public school to a family's living room, a child's behavior has been a way of communicating a need. My role has moved from managing that behavior, to understanding it, to teaching the adults around the child to understand it too: caregivers, preschool staff, and new RBTs.

I am working toward my BCBA, then a doctorate, and eventually teaching women in prison, whose struggles so often begin in childhood. At every step, I want to be the person who helps someone understand how they learn, the way I had to learn it myself.
