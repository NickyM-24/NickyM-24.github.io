The outcomes

OutcomeWhat it asks
IE.A Application of Ethical Perspectives/Concepts Related to EducationIndependently apply ethical perspectives/concepts in an educational environment and consider the full implications of the application.
IE.B Connections to ExperienceSynthesize connections among experiences outside of the formal course (including life experiences and academic experiences such as internships) to deepen understanding of educational contexts (learners, educators, settings), and learning, and to broaden points of view.
IE.C ReflectionReview prior learning (past experiences inside and outside of the classroom) to reveal significantly changed perspectives about educational contexts (learners, educators, settings), learning, and life experiences.
IE.D TransferMake explicit references to previous learning and apply in an innovative way that knowledge and those skills to comprehend and perform in novel situations.
IE.E Self-Awareness of Core Educational BeliefsAnalyze both core beliefs and the origins of the core beliefs related to education, learning, learner, and educator.
IE.F Envisions Future SelfEnvision a future self embedded with values and beliefs that have occurred across multiple and diverse educational contexts and experiences.
