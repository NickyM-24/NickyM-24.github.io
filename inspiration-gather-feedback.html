Inspiration

Gather Feedback

Who saw the prototype

The flier was shared in three settings: a therapist work meeting at Bamboo Sunrise, foster care agency meetings/training sessions, and community centers via an information booth.

Feedback received

  • Mackenzie: the concept was promising, but needed more measurable outcomes — some way to track progress and demonstrate effectiveness.
  • Olivia: wanted more clarity on real-world implementation — a step-by-step guide or case study for therapists.
  • Pau: flagged the need for more inclusive language and accessibility across different therapists and kids' backgrounds.

All three pieces of feedback made it into the next iteration — measurable outcomes were the most directly actionable, implementation clarity the most valuable for adoption, and the accessibility note pushed the design to consider a wider range of settings.
