Inspiration

Rapid Prototype

What to prototype

Mapped out the key moments in a therapeutic relationship dealing with violent tantrums — awareness, support/access, and the experience itself — and landed on "Skill Reinforcement through Therapeutic Activities" as the concept to prototype: hands-on, simulated practice for clients to apply coping skills in a controlled setting rather than just discussing them.

The biggest open question going in: whether this approach would work equally well across clients with very different backgrounds, triggers, and responses.

What was built

The prototype took the form of a flier introducing the Skill Reinforcement concept to therapists and caregivers — something tangible to test reactions and gather feedback on before investing in a fuller program.
