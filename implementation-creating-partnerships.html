Creating Partnerships

Who could help my project reach further, and what each partner would get back. My strategies only hold if the adults around a child respond to a tantrum the same way, and that includes the adults at school.

  • Chosen partner: Clark County School District (CCSD)
  • Where it starts: the two CCSD classrooms where I already work with clients
  • Other possible partners: Child Haven, which A Parentz Touch will soon partner with to take clients, A Parentz Touch ABA Services itself, and local nonprofits that provide clothes, school supplies and sensory items

Why CCSD

We serve the same children. Two of my clients are in CCSD classrooms right now, and CCSD is allowing ABA into the classroom, so we already share the goal of helping students with intense behaviors stay in class and learn.

What I would ask for is small: permission to keep using the same calm down strategies in the classroom, a few minutes to show teachers the strategy and the cue cards, and a spot in the room where a child can calm down. In return, teachers get simple, trauma informed strategies they can use with any student, and a child whose responses are the same at school, at home, and in session.
