Design Challenge

Module readings

The most useful reading for this step was on the problem-solving process: defining and understanding a problem and its causes, then identifying which causal or contextual factors are actually modifiable. Framing the problem this way — as something with modifiable and non-modifiable parts — shaped how the rest of the project was scoped.

Brainstorming with a supervisor

Kicked off the design challenge with a 30-minute meeting with Dr. Janel White Taylor of Stabilization Family Services to brainstorm current challenges and opportunities within the organization — the starting point for what became the project's focus: violent tantrums in the foster system.
