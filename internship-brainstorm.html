Brainstorm

An early brainstorm of the causes behind violent tantrums — the starting point for framing the design challenge — grouped into categories, each with a first pass at possible interventions.

Categories identified

  • Communication issues & learning disorders — inability to cope with or express feelings, short attention span
  • Unexpected changes / unfamiliar environments — not getting enough attention, unfamiliar settings
  • Uncomfortable or stressful environments — invasion of personal space, disagreeing with instructions
  • Bullying & peer conflict — violating peers' rights, sense of injustice or frustration
  • Overstimulation & boredom
  • Emotional trauma — physical, mental, or emotional abuse at home or aggression toward self; fight-or-flight responses; panic attacks; learned behavior

Early intervention ideas

Some of the earliest intervention ideas that came out of this brainstorm — training foster parents to recognize triggers early, individual or group therapy for emotional self-regulation, mindfulness and coping tools like sensory items, open communication between kids and caregivers, trauma-informed therapy, and reducing stigma through community and school awareness — carried through into the final project direction.
