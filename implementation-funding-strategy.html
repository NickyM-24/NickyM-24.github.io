Funding Strategy

What my project costs, where the money comes from, and how I plan to raise what is not already covered. My pilot runs fifteen weeks with two to three caregivers and their children at A Parentz Touch ABA.

  • Total projected expenses: about $781
  • Secured or in-kind: about $450
  • Dependent on fundraising: about $306

The short version

A funding strategy is what gets a solution off the ground, and it is not the same as a long term revenue plan. So I planned only for what my fifteen week pilot actually costs, not for a permanent program.

Most of what I need, the sensory materials, the space, the staff time, already exists at my organization. Working through my capabilities made me realize that APTAS holds most of what this project depends on, which moved several lines from expenses into in-kind support and lowered what I actually have to raise.

My plan is sequenced so the pilot is never blocked waiting on money. Everything essential to running sessions is covered in the first five weeks. The items that depend on fundraising, mainly the printed implementation guide and translation, are not needed until the final phase.
