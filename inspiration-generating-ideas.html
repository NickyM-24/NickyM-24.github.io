Generating Ideas

Themes across the research

Clustering the interview, survey, secondary research, and immersion notes surfaced a consistent set of themes: frequency of tantrums varies widely; ethical, sensitive recruitment matters as much as the questions themselves; caregiver support is central; age-appropriate approaches are non-negotiable; and trauma runs underneath most of it, even when the presenting behavior looks similar.

Insight statement

Secondary research emphasized the complexity of violent tantrums and the importance of addressing underlying causes, not just the behavior itself. The biggest personal takeaway: trust with these kids has to be built gradually, and deeper topics only work once that trust and a supportive, non-invasive atmosphere are in place.

How Might We questions

  • How might we create a safe, supportive environment for kids in the foster system to express and cope with violent tantrums?
  • How might we identify individual triggers and underlying causes to build targeted interventions?
  • How might we collaborate with child welfare agencies and foster parents on a comprehensive support system?
  • How might we train and resource foster parents to de-escalate effectively?
  • How might we use therapeutic techniques and counseling to build emotional regulation skills?
  • How might we reduce stigma and build public understanding of what these kids are dealing with?
  • How might we advocate for policy and funding changes for better mental health support in foster care?
  • How might we involve kids themselves in decisions about their care and tantrum-management strategies?
  • How might we build a peer support network for foster kids facing similar challenges?

Feedback

Reviewed the themes, insights, and HMW questions with mentor Pau Jansa in late October 2023 — a conversation that covered a broader set of survey and interview results (across roughly ten participants). The main takeaway from that meeting: there's no single approach to reducing tantrums — it's an individualized process of understanding each child's specific triggers and what actually works for them.
