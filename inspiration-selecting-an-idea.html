Inspiration

Selecting an Idea

Bundling ideas into clusters

  • Prevention & Early Intervention — training foster parents to recognize early signs, trauma-informed care practices, ongoing therapeutic support.
  • Communication & Coping Skills — teaching kids and caregivers healthy emotional expression, anger management, and mindfulness techniques.
  • Individualized Support Plans — behavioral assessments feeding into personalized intervention plans per child.
  • Crisis Management & De-escalation — training caregivers to safely manage an active tantrum.
  • Ongoing Monitoring & Evaluation — tracking incidents and triggers over time to adjust the approach.

Constraints considered

Limited resources and a shortage of trauma-trained professionals were the two biggest constraints, alongside variability in how ready individual foster parents were to take on intensive training, and the sheer difficulty of collecting sensitive data ethically while protecting privacy.

The decision: Communication & Coping Skills

Of the five clusters, Communication and Coping Skills stood out as the most practical starting point — it's the foundation the other clusters depend on. It's directly transferable across different kids and caregivers, doesn't require specialized clinical staffing to start, and builds the trust needed for individualized plans and crisis management to actually work later.

Feedback

Reviewed and fine-tuned this decision with Pau Jansa and Mackenzie Dewar in early November 2023 — landing on a final project focus: how violent tantrums affect at-risk kids, what triggers them, and how caregivers respond.
