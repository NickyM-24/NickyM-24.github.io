Exploring Scalability

How my project could grow to reach more families and produce more outcomes, and how my supervisor's feedback shaped that plan. My pilot, Skill Reinforcement through Therapeutic Activities, runs at A Parentz Touch ABA.

  • Project: Skill Reinforcement through Therapeutic Activities
  • Site: A Parentz Touch ABA
  • Supervisor: L'Tricia Turner
  • Pilot status: Week 10 of 15, two of three families still taking part

The short version

My pilot currently reaches two foster families through in home sessions, live caregiver coaching, and weekly check in messages. To scale for reach, I would serve more foster families at A Parentz Touch ABA and, once the agency's partnership with Child Haven begins, children from Child Haven. I would reach them through group caregiver workshops and short video examples, and I would extend the work into CCSD classrooms where I already support clients.

To scale for outcomes, I would add three outcomes: fewer behavior incidents at school, caregivers who feel less stressed and more confident, and caregivers who can teach the strategies to other caregivers.

When I met with L'Tricia Turner on October 24, she supported the ideas but advised me to grow slowly, one step at a time. Because of her feedback, I will start inside A Parentz Touch ABA with one more family and one more trained RBT, review my data at each step, and protect the consent and privacy of every child before any work moves into schools or Child Haven.
