The outcomes

OutcomeWhat it asks
LAI.A AdvocacyTake informed and responsible action to address social concerns within an educational system to change a community and/or a learner; evaluate the local and broader consequences of individual and collective interventions.
LAI.B Sustainable SolutionsImplement an educational solution and evaluate the social, environmental, and economic sustainability of the solution.
LAI.C Navigates Supports and BarriersDemonstrate ability and commitment to work with support systems and through barriers when making changes in an educational setting.
LAI.D Designing an Education SolutionDesign an educational system or program that addresses a specific problem/concern in an educational setting. The design shows a deep understanding of the problem, is tailored to the educational community, is research-based, is feasible, and is sustainable.
LAI.E Using a Design ProcessUse a human-centered design process to improve an educational concern/issue.
