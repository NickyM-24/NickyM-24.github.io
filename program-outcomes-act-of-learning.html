OutcomeWhat it asks
AL.A Educational TheoriesAccurately analyze the theory or theories applied when designing an educational environment or educational activity.
AL.B Disciplinary KnowledgeConnect and extend knowledge from one's own academic study/field/discipline when reflecting on the design of an educational environment, and its appropriateness in helping learners acquire the desired knowledge.
