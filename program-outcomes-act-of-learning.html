Act of Learning

How I apply learning theory, disciplinary knowledge, and the right tools when I design learning for children and adults. My Module 6 brainstorm covers Act of Learning and Identity as an Educator together.

  • Experience 1: EDU 498, designing the pilot for my HCD project at A Parentz Touch ABA
  • Experience 2: LSE 401, an inclusion workshop I designed for the preschool staff at Elevate Academy
  • Experience 3: my RBT Trainer role, onboarding and training new RBT hires

The short version

The courses that connect most to this domain are the ones where I had to design something for real learners and explain why I designed it that way. In my pilot, the learners are not only the children. The caregivers and other RBTs have to learn the strategies too, which made me think hard about how adults learn.

Two theories sit underneath the pilot: trauma informed care, especially Dr. Bruce Perry's sequence of regulate, relate, then reason, and applied behavior analysis. When I train new RBTs, I teach the same way ABA teaches skills: I explain the skill, model it, have them practice it, and give feedback right away.
